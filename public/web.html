Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about DomusAI

Can I upload client documents to DomusAI? +

Yes, DomusAI has been designed in accordance with Alter Domus compliance and security measures. You can safely upload both client and internal documents to enhance efficiency in your workflow. The tool is safe to use for all regulated entities within Alter Domus.

What file types are supported? +

DomusAI can process PDFs, CSVs, text documents, Word and Excel files, and images. You can upload files using drag and drop, the Files button, or the Add button in the chat area.

Is my data used for AI training? +

No, your data is never used for model training purposes. All data remains in-house, is encrypted, and is not shared with third parties. Your privacy is completely protected.

How current is the AI's knowledge? +

The GPT-4o Mini model's training data includes information up to October 2023. For more recent information, you can provide documents or context in your queries.

Can I access internal SharePoint documents? +

Integration with internal resources like SharePoint and AD News pages is on our roadmap and will be available in future releases. Currently, you can upload documents directly to DomusAI.

Who is responsible for the content generated? +

Users are solely responsible for checking the accuracy of the outputs generated by AI tools, including DomusAI. Always verify important information before using it in your work.
